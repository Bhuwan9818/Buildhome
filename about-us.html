Who We Are

Dhanyashree Homes — a transformative force in luxury residential and retail real estate in South Delhi.

Founded in 2019, Dhanyashree Homes South Delhi has quickly risen as one of South Delhi's most dynamic and innovative real estate developers, built on the values of ethical governance, transparency, commitment, and passion for excellence.

"A forward-looking vision crafted by Founder Chairman Ms. Apoorva Singh."

Today, Dhanyashree Homes stands as a symbol of sophistication and innovation, setting new benchmarks across India in luxury residential and retail real estate.

Track Record

With an impressive portfolio of 66 projects, Dhanyashree Homes has already delivered all developments launched before 2025. Another 6 projects, covering 10 million square feet, are in full swing — all adhering to the company's unwavering commitment to timely delivery.

Signature projects like Ryhan Heights, Ryhan Residency, Ryhan Square in Vasant Kunj, and King & Queen's in Shantikunj have redefined luxury living and established South Delhi as a premier real estate hub. These developments have earned numerous awards and stand as certified luxury real estate landmarks.

As a distinguished real estate developer and realtor, we specialise in offering expertly designed and certified projects and properties within the South Delhi region.
